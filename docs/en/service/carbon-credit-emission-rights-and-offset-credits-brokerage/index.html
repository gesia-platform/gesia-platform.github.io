Carbon Credit (Emission Rights and Offset Credits) Brokerage

Externally certified carbon offsets are issued in the form of vouchers and voucher derivatives, and these products are offered in the market through decentralized finance (De-Fi) style brokerage services. These derivatives can be bought and sold using users' cold wallets via the Patricia Merkel chain, and all transactions take place on a decentralized exchange (DEX).

The initial market will offer three types of markets: a cash card payment market, a brokerage's own points market, and the NZC ( Net Zero Climate Token) market issued by Gesiaplatform (BVI).

This service is currently in preparation and is scheduled to launch in May 2024.
