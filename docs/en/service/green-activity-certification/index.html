Green Activity Certification

When a member carries out carbon offset activities (either retirement or donation), we plan to issue a special certificate in the form of an NFT (Non-Fungible Token) to verify these actions. This NFT certificate will be awarded to the member as a way to officially recognize and record their participation and contribution to the carbon offset activities.
